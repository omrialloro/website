σ-ALGEBRA Guide
✏Curves ▼
Curves are the scaffolding of your composition. Draw freely on the canvas — your stroke is smoothed, simplified, and converted into a clean bezier path. Spinners then travel along these paths.

Adjust Smooth to soften jagged input, and Simplify to reduce the number of control points. Close a curve by ending near where you started.

◎Spinners ▼
Spinners are rotating arms that travel along curves. Click a curve to attach one, click a spinner's glowing tip dot to chain another on top of it, or click anywhere on the canvas to place a free-floating spinner.

Each spinner has independent speed, arm length, travel rate, and color. Enable Trace to draw a trail from the tip. Use the A buttons in the controller strip to automate speed or length with a sine, square, or saw wave — see Automation ↓.

✦Brush ▼
Brushes paint continuously from a point on any spinner arm or curve. Click while in Brush mode to snap a brush to the nearest point. The brush follows that point as it moves, drawing a stroke over time.

Set two colors and a morph speed to cycle between them. Use Decay to make the paint trail fade away. Automate size or the morph blend from the controller strip — see Automation ↓.

Multiple brushes can be grouped together to share a single set of automation controls — see Grouping ↓.

∿Automation ▼
Every controller card — spinner speed, spinner length, brush size, brush morph, and group knobs — has an A button in the top-left corner. Press it to expand the card and reveal the automation lane.

Each lane shows a live waveform canvas. Use the Amp slider (yellow, right) to set oscillation depth and the DC slider (blue, left) to shift the center value. The Freq slider at the bottom controls cycle speed.

Choose between sine, square, and saw wave shapes. The wave drives the knob continuously — the knob itself shows the live output value so you always know where you are.

⊞Grouping ▼
Grouping lets you collapse multiple brush objects into one shared controller. All member brushes keep their individual attachment points and colors, but their size and morph are driven together from the group card.

To create a group: switch to Brush mode, then Shift+click two or more brush cards in the controller strip. A yellow action bar appears above the strip — hit ⊞ Group to collapse them. The individual cards are replaced by a single GRP card showing color swatches of all members.

The group card has its own size and morph knobs with full A automation support (sine, square, saw) — see Automation ↑. This lets you pulse or oscillate an entire set of brushes in perfect sync with a single wave.

Hit ungroup on the card to dissolve it back to individual cards — brushes keep their last values. Hit ✕ to delete the group and all its member brushes entirely.
